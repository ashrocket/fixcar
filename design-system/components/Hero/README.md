The Hero is the first screen of the Fender Fit Lab landing page: the tool name, a two-sentence description, one primary action and three info pills. The source has no button or link; the action is an addition.

## When to use

Use the Hero as the top band of the landing page, above the 3D viewer. Use it once per page.

## What the consumer supplies

- The page title, `Camaro Fender Fit Lab`.
- The description, two sentences, as in `preview.html`.
- The action label, `Open the fit viewer`, and its link to the viewer page.
- The three pill labels: the asset file name, the viewer mode and the viewer library.

## Do

- Keep the pills as info only, as in the source: Space Grotesk, `--text-dim` on the `--panel-soft` fill (ratio 4.25, a source pair kept exact).
- Keep one primary action: teal fill, `--panel-strong` text, Space Grotesk 600. Do not add a second one.
- Let the title and description wrap. The panel is fluid down to 390 px wide.

## Do not

- Do not describe how the project was built or what changed from an earlier version. Describe what the tool does.
- Do not put `--text-dim` text on the page background. Use it on the panel only (ratio 4.22 on `--bg`).
